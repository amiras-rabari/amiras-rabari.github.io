IoT flood detection system

A board that watches water level, flow and humidity, sounds an alarm locally and sends an alert to your phone. My capstone project at Seneca College, from schematic to firmware.

Watch the demo video

Block diagram

  1. SensorsUltrasonic water-level sensor, float switch, Hall-effect flow sensor and a DHT11 for temperature and humidity.
  2. ATmega328P firmwareEmbedded C with non-blocking acquisition: interrupt-driven pulse counting for flow, pulse timing for the ultrasonic sensor, and the DHT11's single-wire protocol.
  3. Local outputA 20×4 LCD readout and a buzzer alarm.
  4. ESP8266 Wi-FiAT commands over UART and HTTP GET requests to a cloud dashboard, which sends the phone alert.

Hardware

I designed the schematic and the PCB layout, then assembled and debugged the board. It includes a regulated supply that steps 12 V down to 5 V and 3.3 V using a bridge rectifier, a 7805 and an LM1117.

The project shipped with a bill of materials (about $110 CAD) and a technical report covering specifications, theory of operation and maintenance.
